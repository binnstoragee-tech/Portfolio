Every surface has a memory of how it was folded. We spend our time there — in the crease, the pull, the shadow a seam leaves behind.

Pli Studio is a small practice building digital material for fashion, product, and interior brands. We work between physical reference and simulation — every drape starts as a real piece of cloth before it becomes geometry.

Material development
Woven, knit, and grain surfaces built for physically-based render engines.
Fold simulation
Cloth behaviour tuned by hand — drape, tension, and memory, not defaults.
Motion & light
Studio lighting rigs and camera choreography for still and animated work.
